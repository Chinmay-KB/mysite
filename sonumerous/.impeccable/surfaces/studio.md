Targets: src/App.tsx, src/styles.css. Mode: Operate. Gallery is Experience.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A personal image studio where a reference is a reusable creative asset and every result can be revisited.
OWN-WORLD: User-selected clean gallery: paper-white surfaces, charcoal controls, quiet grey divisions, precise humanist typography, one committed sindoor-red accent for actions and active states. Strong image scale, full-bleed tiles with overlaid labels, low UI density. Flat surfaces and understated 12px corners.
STORY: Pick a saved image or describe an idea, generate, and keep refining. Library retains every upload and generated version.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Small left navigation, generous title with a concise description; compact composer beside a large quiet preview. Mobile presents prompt and saved references in a single column, with persistent labelled bottom navigation.
FORM: Clean gallery selected explicitly by user over contact-sheet direction; seed 84503442, canon override. No fabricated history or decorative mock results.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
