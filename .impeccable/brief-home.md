# Surface brief: home (chinmaykabi.com)

Scope: homepage + site-wide world (articles index, article pages, projects inherit).
Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport); article pages are Read.

Audience: Chinmay himself (self-representation) plus peers and friends. Explicitly not recruiters.
Job: feel the person in one viewport; find writing, projects, contact within seconds.
Proof/content: 5 imported dev.to articles, project write-ups (wp_pivot_flutter, translations_cleaner,
stringr, project-spampr, Daydream, ifXBuiltY + shareable Blend work), employment history, resume PDF,
contact links (email, LinkedIn, GitHub, X, dev.to).
Constraints: warm-light ground (pinned), bold expressive world (pinned), lively micro-interactions
(pinned); plain static output via tiny Markdown generator; Cloudflare Workers static assets.
Anti-slop rebuild (v2): triangulates the owner's wp_pivot_flutter Metro panorama with a punk
flyer wall. Uniform cards, pill buttons, identical hovers/reveals, and vague-noun headers are
banned from this surface by decision, not taste.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal site should feel like stepping into the owner's workshop, not reading his resume.
This surface owns the tinkerer's wall: panorama signage bleeding off-canvas, a taped flyer, a dotted
writing ledger, a tilted pegboard where every tool is rendered from its own facts. It refuses the
category default of quiet centered hero plus uniform card grid — and, after a rebuild, refuses its
own first draft's uniform rhythm too.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground, near-black ink, one committed vermilion accent plus ochre and green
section bands and a single amber night dot. Chunky condensed grotesk display, warm slab body, mono
reserved for data. Squared paper (3-4px), peg dots, tape strips, static tilts, offset shadows that
warm or cool by section. Themed selection/caret/scrollbar/focus. No kickers, no gradient text, no
icon-cards, no pills, no sticky chrome.

STORY: The visitor understands in one viewport whose wall this is and what lives on it; believes it
because the projects are specimens with real descriptions and the ledger shows real dates; reaches
articles, projects, or contact in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed panorama name (double, cut off-canvas, -1.5deg) above a claim plus taped
marginalia note with live status; raw tab strip (Field notes / Hung tools / Clocked in / Yell at me);
taped newest-article flyer overlapping into the ledger. Primary action is the ledger, one glance down.

FORM: The Tinkerer's Wall, candidate 4 of 7 grounded derivations, seed key f9e69daf, rebuilt v2 from
Metro-panorama x flyer-wall anchors after an anti-slop verdict. Raised by Dumbar banding, daylight
timestamps, sun pointer-light, seven-seg status, lowbrow hover sheen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
