FlashLite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

FlashLite (package flashkreig.kablitzkreig.flashlite) is a flashlight app by Chinmay Kabi. It turns your phone's flashlight on or off and does nothing else.

Data we collect

None. FlashLite does not collect, store, or share any personal or device data.

  • It requests no Android permissions.
  • It has no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or third-party SDKs.

Children

FlashLite is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before a new version is released.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/Chinmay-KB/flashlite/issues.
